Friction - AI Study Tool from Your Notes

Friction turns your own lecture notes into interactive lessons. Paste notes or upload a PDF and it builds a slideshow, quiz, flashcards and fill-in-the-blank exercises from the actual content — not generic explanations — with some slides carrying an interactive simulation seeded from your own numbers. PDFs are parsed in the browser, falling back to OCR for scans and photos. Named for "desirable difficulty" in learning science: material you work at is material you retain. A signed-out demo runs a full lesson, no account needed.

RippleGuard - Credit-Risk Stress Simulator

Winner of Challenge 3 at the Innovate AI Hackathon 2026, run with BNZ and AWS. It models a bank's lending book as a graph, not a table, so a sector shock reaches suppliers and guarantors never hit directly — before anyone misses a payment. Scoring is deterministic with no LLM in the loop, so every number is reproducible and every flag cites its policy clause. Overrides need a written justification that Amazon Comprehend checks is substantive. Team of three on synthetic data; I owned the AWS backend, deploy tooling and two frontend screens.

Python AWS Lambda DynamoDB API Gateway S3 IAM Comprehend NetworkX React TypeScript

Dazey - AI Journal & Note Taker

Dazey is an intelligent AI powered, privacy-first journaling and note-taking app that helps bring clarity and structure to your thoughts. Using local AI (Gemma 3n 4b), it organizes raw thoughts, notes, and reflections into smart categories, detects habit patterns, and includes a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot for searching past notes. Built for the GDGCxDEVS Hackathon 2025 by a team of me along with five other second-year students, Dazey won First Place as well as the Emerging Talent award.
